About

I am Soheyb Alimoradi, a Computer Engineering graduate from the University of Kurdistan. I completed my Bachelor's degree in 2026 and am now preparing for the next stage of my academic journey.

Throughout my Bachelor's studies, I explored several areas of computer engineering. Alongside my coursework, I worked as a teaching assistant and gained practical experience as an intern, including work on a machine vision project.

Artificial intelligence has become one of the areas I am most interested in pursuing further. I have worked on several AI projects, and I would like to deepen my understanding by studying emerging and frontier topics in the field.

An especially important part of my undergraduate experience was a six-month scholarship at the University of Jaén in Spain. Studying in a different academic environment gave me a valuable international perspective and became an important part of my personal and academic development.

I am currently exploring Master's opportunities and have not yet decided on a specific field or university. For now, my main goal is to continue learning, build stronger research foundations, and find an area of AI and computer engineering that I can explore more deeply.
